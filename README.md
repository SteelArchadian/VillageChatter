# Village Chatter

**Talk to villagers instead of jumping straight into trades.** Right-click a villager, guard, wandering trader, pillager or piglin and a dialogue box opens, with mumbled voices, subtitles and choices that matter. Your reputation, the village's laws and the people on the road all remember what you do.

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1+ · Java 21 · version 0.16.25-alpha**

> **Alpha:** it's playable, but expect bugs and rough edges. It's posted now so people can test it. **Feedback, bug reports and feature ideas are very welcome**: please post them in **[Issues](https://github.com/SteelArchadian/VillageChatter/issues)**. Every one gets read, and feature requests are genuinely encouraged.

## What you get

- **Conversations:** a dark, see-through dialogue box with rounded choice buttons, text that scrolls in while the speaker mumbles in their own voice, and a camera that turns to face them. Item names show in their rarity colour (modded rarities too), enchanted items shimmer, and places show in teal.
- **Who you can talk to:** villagers and their kids, guards, wandering traders, iron golems, your pets, pillagers and piglins.
- **Reputation and factions:** your standing with each villager, each village, the pillagers and the piglins. Look at a villager or a bell to see where you stand. Gifts (every profession has its own tastes), piglin barter by dialogue, and slow, honest reputation that fades unless you keep helping.
- **Village law:** crimes, witnesses, fines, bribes and bounties. Guards accost you, chase you, and remember. Allies forgive the first accidental hit.
- **Living villages:** villagers chat with each other, guards call out in fights and on night watch, and friends sometimes help you out.
- **Quests and errands:** villagers with a problem bring it up when you talk to them, from kids who want flowers to heirlooms lost to a guardian in a nearby cave.
- **Encounters on the road:**
  - merchant and traveler caravans, some with supply carts;
  - pillager holdups you can pay, talk down, bluff or fight;
  - outpost lookouts who demand a toll;
  - pillager caravans with captives to free;
  - bounty collectors sent by villages you've wronged, and hired thugs.
- **Sneak + right-click** skips the dialogue and trades as normal.

## Compatibility

- Works with any mob that extends vanilla's villager class, so most modded villagers and traders work out of the box. Mobs can be added with the `#villagechatter:talkable` entity tag; Guard Villagers' guards are included.
- Runs at low event priority, so any mod that already handles a villager right-click wins.
- Optional: Config Panel, for a polished in-game settings screen. Everything is also in `config/villagechatter-*.toml`.

## Adding dialogue (no code needed)

Lines live in lang files as `villagechatter.<topic>.<pool>.<n>`. A resource pack can add lines for modded professions, for example `"villagechatter.greeting.profession.morevillagers.florist.0": "Lovely day for tulips!"`. Pools include `generic`, `profession.<ns>.<path>`, `entity.<ns>.<path>`, `guard`, `night`, `rain`, `hero`, `grumpy`, `combat`, `victory` and `danger`; topics are `greeting`, `smalltalk` and `farewell`.

## Building

```
./gradlew build
```

The jar ends up in `build/libs/`. The UI sounds are generated by `tools/sound_gen/generate.py` (numpy, scipy, ffmpeg).
